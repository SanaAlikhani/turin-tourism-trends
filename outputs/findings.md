## Read with care

- The data are provincial totals (Province of Turin), not figures for the city alone.
- The dataset gives totals only. It does not explain the large jumps (for example the rise between 2004 and 2005, or between 2008 and 2009), so this analysis does not attribute causes. Check the methodology of the original ARPA Piemonte indicator before drawing conclusions about events such as the 2006 Winter Olympics.
- A reported rise in arrivals per business means busier businesses on average, not necessarily higher profit or occupancy.
